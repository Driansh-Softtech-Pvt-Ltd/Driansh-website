The command bar is a search box for actions. Press a key, type a few letters, and press Enter. You can jump to any page or change the conversation you're looking at without reaching for the mouse.

## Open the command bar

- Press **⌘ + K** on a Mac or **Ctrl + K** on Windows.
- A box appears with the hint **Search or jump to**.
- Start typing to filter the list. Use **↑** and **↓** to move, and **Enter** to choose.
- Press **Esc** to close it.

Some commands open a second list. For example, **Assign an agent** shows your agents. Pick one and press **Enter**.

## What you can do

The list changes depending on where you are and what you have selected.

### Go anywhere

Available on every page, grouped under **General**, **Reports** and **Settings**:

| Group | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | **Go to My Inbox**, **Go to Conversation Dashboard**, **Go to Contacts Dashboard**, **Go to Campaigns**, **Go to Help Center** |
| **Reports** | **Go to Reports Overview**, **Go to Conversation Reports**, **Go to Agent Reports**, **Go to CSAT Reports**, **Go to SLA Reports** |
| **Settings** | **Go to Agent Settings**, **Go to Inbox Settings**, **Go to Label Settings**, **Go to Macros**, **Go to Canned Response Settings**, **Go to Profile Settings** |

You only see pages your role can open and that your plan includes.

### Act on the open conversation

When a conversation is open, you can also:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Resolve conversation** / **Reopen conversation** | Changes the status |
| **Snooze Conversation** | Hides it until a time you choose, such as **Until next reply**, **Until tomorrow**, **Until next week** or **Custom...** |
| **Assign an agent** | Picks the agent who owns it |
| **Assign a team** | Moves it to a team |
| **Assign priority** | Sets the priority |
| **Add label to the conversation** | Adds a label. Labels appear with a `#` in front |
| **Remove label from the conversation** | Removes a label |
| **Execute a macro** | Runs one of your macros |
| **Mute conversation** / **Unmute conversation** | Stops or restarts notifications for it |
| **Send an email transcript** | Emails a copy of the conversation |
| **AI Assist** | Rewrites or summarises your draft with the EngageOne AI Assistant, if it's turned on for your account |

> **Tip:** When you snooze from the command bar, you can type a time in plain words, such as "2 hours", "tomorrow" or "next friday". EngageOne suggests matching times for you to pick.

### Act on selected conversations

If you've ticked conversations in the list, the command bar adds **Bulk Actions**: snooze, resolve or reopen all of them at once. See [Bulk actions](/docs/productivity/bulk-actions).

### Other commands

- **Change Appearance**: switch between **Light**, **Dark** and **System** themes.
- **Snooze Notification**: on **My Inbox**, snooze the notification you're looking at.

## Make it a habit

- Use **⌘ + K** or **Ctrl + K**, type `assign`, and pick **Assign an agent** instead of opening the assignee dropdown.
- Type `label` to reach **Add label to the conversation**, then type part of the label name.
- Type `macro` to reach **Execute a macro** and see the macros you can run on this conversation.
- Type `go to` and a page name to move around settings and reports.
