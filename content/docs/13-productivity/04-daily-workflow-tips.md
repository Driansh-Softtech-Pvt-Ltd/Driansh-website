These tips help you and your team keep the inbox under control every day. They combine features you already have in EngageOne, so there's nothing new to set up beyond a few folders and templates.

## Start your day in the right place

1. Set your status to **Online** from your avatar menu, so the right conversations reach you.
2. Open **Mentions** first. These are teammates waiting on you, and they're usually quick to answer.
3. Open **Unattended**. These customers haven't had a reply to their latest message yet.
4. Switch to the **Mine** tab and set **Order by** to **Pending Response: Longest first**. Work from the top down.
5. Finally, check **Unassigned** and pick up anything that fits your skills.

> **Tip:** Set your status to **Busy** when you need focus time, and **Offline** when you leave. That keeps auto-assignment from sending you chats you can't answer.

## Build a few folders, then live in them

A folder is a saved filter. It updates by itself, so you don't have to rebuild the same filter every morning.

Good folders to start with:

| Folder | Filters |
| --- | --- |
| **My urgent** | Assignee is you, priority is **Urgent** or **High**, status is **Open** |
| **VIP customers** | Label is `vip`, status is **Open** |
| **Waiting on us** | Team is yours, status is **Open** |
| **Follow up today** | Label is `follow-up`, status is **Snoozed** or **Pending** |

To create one, filter the list, then click **Save filter**. It appears under **Conversations → Folders**. See [Conversation filters and folders](/docs/features/conversation-filters).

## Stop typing the same answer

- Turn your five most common replies into canned responses. Type `/` and a short code in the reply box to insert one.
- Use variables such as `{{contact.first_name}}` so templates still feel personal.
- Keep short codes easy to guess, for example `/refund`, `/hours`, `/tracking`.
- Review your list once a month. Delete templates nobody uses and fix ones agents always edit.

See [Canned responses](/docs/features/canned-responses).

## Turn repeat routines into macros

If you do the same three clicks on many conversations, make it a macro. For example:

- **Refund approved**: send the refund reply, add the `refund` label, resolve.
- **Escalate to billing**: assign the Billing team, set priority to **High**, add a private note.
- **Spam**: add the `spam` label, resolve.

Run a macro from the **Macros** section of the conversation details panel, or press **⌘ + K** / **Ctrl + K** and choose **Execute a macro**. See [Macros](/docs/features/macros).

## Snooze instead of leaving it open

When you're waiting on a customer or a teammate, don't leave the conversation sitting in your open list.

- Snooze **Until next reply** when the customer owes you an answer. The conversation comes back the moment they reply.
- Snooze **Until tomorrow** or **Next week** when you've promised to follow up on a date.
- Use **Mark as pending** when the next step is with someone else and you want it out of your open view.

A snoozed conversation also reopens when the customer writes back, so you never miss their reply.

## Use mentions instead of side chats

- Ask a colleague for help with a private note and an `@mention`. They get notified, and the question stays with the conversation.
- Mention a whole team, such as `@Billing`, when you don't know who should answer.
- When you hand over a conversation, leave a one-line private note: what the customer wants, what you've checked, what's next.

See [Private notes and mentions](/docs/features/private-notes-and-mentions).

## Keep your hands on the keyboard

Learn these five first:

| Action | Mac | Windows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next conversation | ⌥ + K | Alt + K |
| Resolve and open the next one | ⌘ + ⌥ + E | Ctrl + Alt + E |
| Switch to private note | ⌥ + P | Alt + P |
| Switch back to reply | ⌥ + L | Alt + L |
| Open the command bar | ⌘ + K | Ctrl + K |

The full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/productivity/keyboard-shortcuts).

## End your day cleanly

1. Resolve anything that's finished. Ask the customer first if you're not sure.
2. Snooze conversations you can't finish today, with a private note saying why.
3. Reassign anything urgent to someone who's still online. Use [bulk actions](/docs/productivity/bulk-actions) if there are several.
4. Set your status to **Offline**.
