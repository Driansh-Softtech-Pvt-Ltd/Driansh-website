---
title: "Refresh your browser"
description: "Hard refresh EngageOne in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, and clear its site data when pages look broken or out of date after an update."
---

A hard refresh loads the latest version of EngageOne instead of an older copy saved in your browser. Try it first whenever something looks wrong after an update.

## When to refresh

- A page looks broken, or buttons are missing or in the wrong place.
- A new feature your team mentioned doesn't appear for you.
- The dashboard keeps loading, or shows an old version of a setting you just changed.
- Your website's chat widget still shows old colours or text after you changed its settings.

## Hard refresh

A hard refresh reloads the page and ignores the saved copy.

| Browser | Windows and Linux | Mac |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chrome | `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `R` | `Cmd` + `Shift` + `R` |
| Edge | `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `R` | `Cmd` + `Shift` + `R` |
| Firefox | `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `R` | `Cmd` + `Shift` + `R` |
| Safari | – | `Cmd` + `Option` + `R` |

You can also hold `Shift` and click the reload button in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

> **Tip:** If you have EngageOne open in several tabs, refresh each of them, or close all but one.

## Clear site data for EngageOne

If a hard refresh doesn't help, clear the data your browser stores for your EngageOne address. This doesn't delete any conversations or settings. They're stored in EngageOne, not in your browser.

### Chrome and Edge

1. Open EngageOne.
2. Click the icon to the left of the address in the address bar.
3. Open **Site settings** in Chrome, or **Permissions for this site** in Edge.
4. Click **Delete data** or **Clear data**, and confirm.
5. Reload the page and sign in again.

### Firefox

1. Open EngageOne.
2. Click the padlock icon to the left of the address.
3. Click **Clear cookies and site data**, and confirm.
4. Reload the page and sign in again.

### Safari on Mac

1. Choose **Safari → Settings** and open the **Privacy** tab.
2. Click **Manage Website Data**.
3. Search for your EngageOne address, select it and click **Remove**.
4. Reload the page and sign in again.

Menu names can change slightly between browser versions.

## After clearing site data

Clearing site data resets a few things that are stored in your browser:

- You're signed out and need to sign in again.
- Browser push notifications are switched off. Turn them on again in your profile. See [Browser notifications](/docs/troubleshooting/browser-notifications).
- Small personal preferences kept in this browser, such as some layout choices, go back to their defaults.

## Still not working?

- Try a private or incognito window. If EngageOne works there, a browser extension may be interfering. Turn off ad blockers and privacy extensions for your EngageOne address.
- Update your browser to the latest version.
- Try another browser.
- If the problem is only on your website's chat widget, refresh your website, not the dashboard. Visitors see widget changes the next time they load a page.

If the problem continues, contact your administrator or Driansh support. Tell them which browser you use, what you see, and when it started.
