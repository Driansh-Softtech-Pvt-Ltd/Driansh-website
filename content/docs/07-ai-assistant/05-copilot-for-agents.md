EngageOne gives agents AI help inside each conversation, so you can reply faster and stay consistent.

## Requirements

- AI features must be turned on for your workspace and an AI provider must be connected. See [Introduction to the EngageOne AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant/introduction).
- The **Copilot** panel also needs at least one assistant. See [Create an assistant](/docs/ai-assistant/create-an-assistant).

If you don't see the AI options described below, ask your administrator.

## AI actions in the reply box

1. Open a conversation.
2. In the reply box, click the sparkle icon above the text area.
3. Choose an action:

| Action | When it appears | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Suggest a reply** | When you're writing a reply (not a private note). | Drafts a reply based on the conversation. |
| **Improve reply** | When your draft has text. | Rewrites your draft so it reads more clearly. |
| **Change tone** | When your draft has text. | Rewrites your draft as **Professional**, **Casual**, **Straightforward**, **Confident** or **Friendly**. |
| **Fix grammar & spelling** | When your draft has text. | Corrects mistakes without changing your meaning. |
| **Summarize the conversation** | In replies and private notes. | Creates a short summary of the conversation. |
| **Ask Copilot** | Always. | Opens the Copilot panel. |

4. Review the result. Edit it if needed before you send.

> **Important:** AI can make mistakes. Always read a suggested reply before you send it to a customer.

## Ask Copilot

Copilot is a chat panel next to the conversation. You can ask it questions in plain language.

1. Open a conversation.
2. Click the **Copilot** icon at the top right of the conversation to open the panel.
3. Pick one of the suggested prompts, or type your own question and press Enter.
4. Click **Use this** to copy a suggested answer into your reply box.
5. Click the **Reset** icon to start a new Copilot chat.

The suggested prompts include:

- **Summarize this conversation**
- **Suggest an answer**
- **Rate this conversation**
- **High priority conversations**
- **List contacts**

You can also ask follow-up questions, for example "Make that shorter" or "What did this customer ask about last week?". If you have more than one assistant, use **Select Assistant** to choose which assistant's knowledge Copilot uses.

> **Tip:** Click **Show steps** to see how Copilot found its answer.

## Label suggestions

EngageOne can suggest labels for a conversation that has no labels yet.

To turn label suggestions on:

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → OpenAI**.
2. Enter your **API Key** if it isn't already set.
3. Tick **Show label suggestions** and save.

When suggestions are available, they appear inside the conversation:

- Click a suggested label to select it, then click **Add selected labels**.
- Or click **Add all labels**.
- Click **Dismiss** to hide the suggestions for that conversation.

See [Labels](/docs/features/labels) to create the labels your team uses.
