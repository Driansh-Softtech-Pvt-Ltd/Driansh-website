The CSAT report shows how satisfied customers are with your support, based on the surveys they answer after a conversation.

## Before you start

CSAT results only appear after you turn on CSAT surveys for your inboxes and customers start answering them. See [CSAT surveys](/docs/features/csat-surveys).

## Open the CSAT report

1. Go to **Reports → CSAT**.
2. Choose a date range at the top of the page. The report shows the last 7 days by default.
3. Optionally, click **Add filter** to narrow the results.

## Summary metrics

| Metric | Meaning | How it's calculated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Total responses** | Number of survey answers received. | Count of responses. |
| **Satisfaction score** | Share of positive ratings. | Ratings of 4 or 5 ÷ total responses × 100. |
| **Response rate** | Share of surveys customers answered. | Responses ÷ surveys sent × 100. |

The **Rating distribution** shows how many customers chose each rating, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

> **Tip:** A low response rate can make the satisfaction score less reliable. If few customers answer, review when and how your survey is sent.

## Filters

Click **Add filter** and choose one or more of:

| Filter | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | See ratings for conversations handled by specific agents. |
| **Inbox** | Compare channels, for example live chat against WhatsApp. |
| **Team** | See ratings for a team. This filter appears only if you use teams. |
| **Rating** | Show only certain ratings, for example all 1 and 2 ratings to follow up on unhappy customers. |

Click **Clear all** to remove every filter.

## Individual responses

Below the summary, a table lists each response with:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | The customer who answered. |
| **Rating** | The rating they gave. |
| **Feedback comment** | Any comment they added. Shows "No feedback provided" if they left none. |
| **Handled by** | The agent assigned to the conversation. |

Click the conversation number next to the contact to open the conversation and see what happened.

### Review notes

If review notes are available on your plan, you can expand a row and add **Review notes**, for example what went wrong and what you did about it. Click **Save**. Other administrators can see who last updated the note.

## Download CSAT data

1. Set the date range and filters you need.
2. Click **Download CSAT Reports** at the top right.

The file downloads in CSV format.

## Ways to use CSAT results

- Filter by **Rating** each week and follow up with unhappy customers.
- Compare inboxes to find channels where customers are less happy.
- Use positive feedback to recognise agents in team meetings.
- Check the **Satisfied customers** tile on the [Overview](/docs/reports/overview-and-live-view) page for a quick trend.
