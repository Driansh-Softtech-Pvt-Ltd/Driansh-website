The **Overview** report shows what's happening in your support right now and how your team performed over recent days.

## Who can see reports

Reports are available to **Administrators** and to custom roles that have permission to manage reports. Agents without this permission don't see the **Reports** menu. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/account-setup/roles-and-permissions).

## Reports at a glance

Open **Reports** in the left sidebar. You'll find these reports:

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Live counts, agent status, traffic heatmaps and a performance summary. |
| **Conversations** | Conversation and message volumes, response and resolution times over time. |
| **Agents** | Performance for each agent. |
| **Labels** | Performance for each label. |
| **Inbox** | Performance for each inbox. |
| **Team** | Performance for each team. |
| **CSAT** | Customer satisfaction survey results. |
| **SLA** | Conversations with service level policies applied, if SLA is available on your workspace. |
| **Bot** | How many conversations your bots handled, resolved and handed off. |

## Live view

Open **Reports → Overview**. The top of the page updates live and shows the current state of your workspace.

![The live Open Conversations and Agent status cards on the Overview report](/docs/images/reports/overview-and-live-view-1.jpg)

### Open conversations

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Conversations that are open right now. |
| **Unattended** | Open conversations where the customer is waiting for a reply from your team. |
| **Unassigned** | Open conversations without an agent. |
| **Pending** | Conversations in the **Pending** status, for example ones the AI Assistant or a bot is handling. |

If you use teams, choose a team from the menu on this card to see its numbers only, or choose **All Teams**.

### Agent status

Shows how many agents are **Online**, **Busy** and **Offline** right now.

### Conversations by agents and teams

Two tables list each agent and each team with their **Open** and **Unattended** conversations, so you can spot who is overloaded.

> **Tip:** Keep the Overview page open on a shared screen during busy periods to watch the queue in real time.

## Performance summary

The **Performance** section shows how your team handled conversations across all channels.

1. Choose a period: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**.
2. Read the tiles. Each one compares the period with the previous period of the same length.
   ![Performance tiles for the last 30 days and the Conversations created vs resolved chart](/docs/images/reports/overview-and-live-view-2.jpg)

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | Conversations in the period. |
| **Avg first response** | Average time until the first reply from your team. |
| **Resolution rate** | Share of conversations that were resolved. |
| **Avg resolution time** | Average time to resolve a conversation. |
| **Satisfied customers** | Share of positive CSAT ratings, with the share of surveys answered. |

Below the tiles you'll find:

- **Channel mix** – conversations per day by channel, and each channel's share.
- **Response time by channel** – average first response time for each channel.
- **Agent performance** – top agents by conversations resolved, with their assigned and resolved counts, average first response, average resolution time and CSAT.

## Traffic and resolution heatmaps

The **Conversation Traffic** and **Resolutions** heatmaps show which days and hours are busiest.

1. Choose a date range, for example **Last 7 days**.
2. Optionally, filter by inbox.
3. Hover over a cell to see the number of conversations in that hour.
4. Click the download button to save the data as a CSV file.

Use these heatmaps to plan shifts and set [business hours](/docs/account-setup/business-hours-and-away-messages).
