This tour shows you what each part of the EngageOne dashboard is for, so you know where to go for every task.

## The layout at a glance

The dashboard has two main parts:

- **The left sidebar** – takes you to each section of EngageOne.
- **The main area** – shows the section you picked, such as your conversation list or a report.

What you see in the sidebar depends on your role. Administrators see every section. Agents see the sections they need to handle conversations. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/account-setup/roles-and-permissions).

> **Tip:** Press **Cmd + K** (Mac) or **Ctrl + K** (Windows) anywhere in the dashboard to open the command bar. Start typing to jump to a page or run an action on the open conversation.

## Sidebar sections

### My Inbox

Your personal notification feed. It lists events that need your attention, such as a conversation assigned to you or a colleague mentioning you in a note. Click an item to jump straight to the conversation.

### Conversations

Where your team spends most of its time.

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All Conversations** | Every conversation you have access to. |
| **Mentions** | Conversations where someone @mentioned you in a private note. |
| **Participating** | Conversations you take part in, even if they are assigned to someone else. |
| **Unattended** | Conversations waiting for a reply from your team. |
| **Folders** | Saved conversation filters you created. |
| **Teams** | Conversations assigned to each team you belong to. |
| **Channels** | One entry per inbox, such as your website chat or WhatsApp number. |
| **Labels** | Conversations tagged with each label. |

### AI Assistant

Set up and train the EngageOne AI Assistant. Here you create assistants, add documents and FAQs, describe scenarios, connect tools, test replies in a playground and choose which inboxes the assistant works in. See [Introduction to the AI Assistant](/docs/ai-assistant/introduction).

### Calls

If voice calling is turned on for your account, this section lists your phone and WhatsApp calls. See [Calling overview](/docs/voice/calling-overview).

### Contacts

Your shared customer list. Use **All Contacts**, **Active** (contacts online right now), **Segments** (saved contact filters) and **Tagged with** (contacts by label). Click a contact to see their details, notes and past conversations. See [Contacts and segments](/docs/features/contacts-and-segments).

### Companies

Groups your contacts by the company they work for, so you can see everyone from one customer organisation together.

### Reports

Numbers about your conversations and team: an **Overview** with live figures, plus reports for **Conversations**, **Agents**, **Labels**, **Inbox**, **Team**, **CSAT**, **SLA** and **Bot**. See [Reports overview and live view](/docs/reports/overview-and-live-view).

### Campaigns

Send messages first. **Live chat** campaigns show a message to visitors on your website. **SMS** and **WhatsApp** campaigns send a one-off message to a group of contacts.

### Help Center

Write and publish help articles for your customers. Manage **Articles**, **Categories**, **Locales** (languages) and **Settings**. See [Set up a help center](/docs/help-center/set-up-a-help-center).

### Settings

Configure your account. Administrators see items such as **Account Settings**, **Agents**, **Teams**, **Inboxes**, **Labels**, **Custom Attributes**, **Automation**, **Bots**, **Macros**, **Canned Responses**, **Integrations**, **Audit Logs**, **Custom Roles**, **SLA**, **Conversation Workflow** and **Security**. Some items appear only when your plan includes them.

### Your profile menu

Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar to:

- Set your availability (**Online**, **Busy** or **Offline**).
- Open **Profile settings**.
- See **Keyboard shortcuts**.
- **Change appearance** (light or dark theme).
- **Switch account**, if you belong to more than one.
- **Log out**.

## The conversation view

When you open **Conversations**, the main area splits into three panes.

![The EngageOne conversation view with the conversation list, the message thread and the contact details panel](/docs/images/getting-started/dashboard-tour-1.jpg)

### 1. Conversation list (left)

- **Tabs** at the top: **Mine** (assigned to you), **Unassigned** and **All**.
- **Status filter**: show **Open**, **Pending**, **Snoozed**, **Resolved** or **All** conversations.
- **Sort and filter** options to narrow the list, for example by inbox, label or date. See [Conversation filters](/docs/features/conversation-filters).
- Each card shows the contact, the inbox, the latest message and how long ago it arrived.

> **Tip:** Hover over a conversation card and tick its checkbox to select several conversations. You can then assign, label, resolve or snooze them in one go.

### 2. Message thread (middle)

- **Header**: the contact's name and the inbox, plus the **Resolve** button. The arrow next to it lets you **Snooze** the conversation or **Mark as pending**.
- **Messages**: the full history with the customer, including attachments, bot replies and private notes (shown with a different background).
- **Reply box** at the bottom with two tabs:
  - **Reply** – sends a message to the customer.
  - **Private Note** – adds a note only your team can see.
- Reply box tools let you add emojis, attach files, insert canned responses (type `/`) and, where available, use AI help to improve your text.

### 3. Details panel (right)

Open or close it from the conversation header. It contains collapsible sections:

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact details** | See and edit the contact's name, email, phone and other details. |
| **Conversation Actions** | Change the assigned agent, team and priority, and add or remove labels. |
| **Macros** | Run a saved set of actions with one click. |
| **Conversation Information** | See technical details such as the browser, page and time the chat started. |
| **Contact Attributes** | View and edit custom attributes for the contact. |
| **Contact Notes** | Read and add notes about the contact. |
| **Previous Conversations** | Open the contact's earlier conversations. |
| **Attachments** | See files shared in the conversation. |
