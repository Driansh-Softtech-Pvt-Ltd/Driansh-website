Driansh EngageOne brings every customer conversation into one shared inbox, so your whole team can reply from a single place.

## What EngageOne does

Your customers message you in many places: your website, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, email and more. Without a shared tool, those messages end up spread across phones, mailboxes and apps.

EngageOne collects them in one dashboard. Each message becomes a **conversation** that your team can read, assign, discuss and resolve. The customer keeps using the app they like. Your team works in one place.

## Who it is for

EngageOne is built for teams that talk to customers every day, for example:

- **Support teams** that answer questions and fix problems.
- **Sales teams** that reply to leads from ads, the website and WhatsApp.
- **Operations teams** in restaurants, clinics, shops and service businesses that take bookings and orders over chat.
- **Contact centers** that handle high volumes across many channels.

## Main features

| Feature | What it gives you |
| --- | --- |
| Shared inbox | Website chat, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, LINE, TikTok, email, SMS and custom API channels in one list. |
| Voice calling | Take and make phone and WhatsApp calls next to your chats (where your plan and channel support it). |
| Website live chat | A chat widget for your website that you can brand and secure. |
| EngageOne AI Assistant | An AI agent that answers common questions from your documents and FAQs, and hands over to a person when needed. |
| Collaboration | Assign conversations, work in teams, add private notes and @mention colleagues. |
| Productivity tools | Labels, canned responses, macros, automation rules and keyboard shortcuts. |
| Contacts | A shared customer list with conversation history, notes and custom attributes. |
| Campaigns | Proactive messages on your website, and one-off campaigns over SMS and WhatsApp. |
| Help Center | A public knowledge base your customers can search before they contact you. |
| Reports | Conversation, agent, inbox, team, label, CSAT and SLA reports. |

> **Note:** Some features, such as the AI Assistant, custom roles, SLA policies and voice calling, depend on your plan. If you can't see a feature described in these docs, ask your administrator or contact Driansh.

## How EngageOne is organised

A few building blocks make up every EngageOne account:

1. **Account** – your company's workspace. It holds your inboxes, team members and settings.
2. **Inboxes** – one inbox for each channel you connect, such as "Website chat" or "WhatsApp – Sales".
3. **Agents and administrators** – the people on your team who reply to customers.
4. **Teams** – groups of agents, such as "Billing" or "Sales", that you can assign conversations to.
5. **Conversations and contacts** – the messages you receive and the people who send them.

The [glossary](/docs/getting-started/glossary) explains each term in more detail.

## Ways to use EngageOne

- **In the browser** – sign in to your EngageOne dashboard from any modern browser.
- **On your phone** – the EngageOne mobile apps for iOS and Android let you reply and get notifications on the go. See [Profile and notifications](/docs/getting-started/profile-and-notifications).
- **Cloud or self-hosted** – Driansh can run EngageOne for you in the Driansh cloud, or install it on your own servers.
