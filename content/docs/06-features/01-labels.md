---
title: "Labels"
description: "Create labels in EngageOne to tag conversations and contacts, filter your inbox, trigger automations and see label reports."
---

Labels are short tags you put on conversations and contacts, such as `billing`, `refund` or `vip`. They help your team sort work, find chats fast and report on topics.

## Create a label

1. Go to **Settings → Labels → Add label**.
2. Fill in the form:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Label Name** | A short name, such as `refund-request`. |
| **Description** | What the label is for, so teammates use it the same way. |
| **Color** | A colour that makes the label easy to spot in lists. |
| **Show label on sidebar** | Tick to add a shortcut for this label in the left sidebar. |

3. Click **Create**.

### Naming rules

- Names are saved in lowercase.
- Use letters, numbers, hyphens (`-`) and underscores (`_`). Spaces are not allowed.
- Each name must be unique in your account.

> **Tip:** Agree on a short list of labels with your team. A few clear labels are more useful than many similar ones.

## Edit or delete a label

1. Go to **Settings → Labels**.
2. Find the label in the list. Use the search box if you have many.
3. Click the edit icon to change it, or the delete icon to remove it.

> **Important:** Deleting a label removes it from the list of labels you can choose. Check your automations, macros and saved folders first, as they may use it.

## Add labels to a conversation

1. Open the conversation.
2. In the right-hand details panel, open **Conversation Labels**.
3. Click to add a label, then choose one or more labels from the list.

To remove a label, click the small cross on it.

### Label many conversations at once

1. In the conversation list, select the conversations you want.
2. In the bulk actions bar, choose **Assign labels**.
3. Pick the labels and confirm.

You can use **Remove labels** in the same way.

## Add labels to a contact

Contact labels describe the person, not one conversation, for example `wholesale` or `trial-user`.

1. Go to **Contacts** and open a contact.
2. Add labels in the contact's details.

## Find conversations and contacts by label

- **Sidebar:** Labels with **Show label on sidebar** turned on appear under **Conversations**. Click one to see every conversation with that label. Under **Contacts → Tagged with**, you see contacts with that label.
- **Filters:** In the conversation list, add a filter on **Labels**. See [Conversation filters and folders](/docs/features/conversation-filters).

## Where else labels are used

| Feature | How it uses labels |
| --- | --- |
| [Automations](/docs/features/automations) | Add or remove labels automatically, or use labels as a condition. |
| [Macros](/docs/features/macros) | Add or remove labels with one click. |
| [CSAT surveys](/docs/features/csat-surveys) | Send surveys only when a conversation has (or does not have) certain labels. |
| Reports | **Reports → Labels** shows conversation volume and response times per label. |
| Website widget | Your website code can add a label to the visitor's conversation. |
