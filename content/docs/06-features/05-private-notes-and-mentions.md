---
title: "Private notes and mentions"
description: "Leave internal notes in EngageOne conversations that customers never see, and @mention teammates or teams to bring them in."
---

Private notes let your team talk inside a conversation without the customer seeing it. Use them to share context, ask for help or leave a hand-over message.

## Write a private note

1. Open a conversation.
2. Above the reply box, switch from **Reply** to **Private Note**.
3. Type your note.
4. Click **Add Note**.
   ![The reply box switched to Private Note, with the note area highlighted in yellow](/docs/images/features/private-notes-and-mentions-1.jpg)

The note appears in the conversation with a different background colour and the text "Private Note: Only visible to you and your team".

> **Tip:** Press **Alt + P** to switch to **Private Note**, and **Alt + L** to switch back to **Reply**. On a Mac, use **Option** instead of **Alt**.

> **Important:** Always check which tab is selected before you send. Text sent from the **Reply** tab goes to the customer.

## Mention a teammate or team

Mentions notify people so they look at the conversation.

1. Switch to **Private Note**.
2. Type `@` and start typing a name.
3. Choose an agent or a team from the list.
4. Finish your note and click **Add Note**.

When you mention a team, every member of that team is notified.

### What happens after a mention

- The mentioned person gets a notification in EngageOne. Depending on their notification settings, they may also get an email or push notification.
- The conversation appears in their **Conversations → Mentions** list, so they can find it later.

Mentions only work in private notes. Customers never see them.

## Notes on a contact

Private notes belong to one conversation. To keep notes about the person across all their conversations, use contact notes instead:

1. Go to **Contacts** and open the contact.
2. Open the **Notes** tab.
3. Type your note and click **Save note**.

You can also see contact notes in the **Contact Notes** section of the conversation details panel.

## Good uses for private notes

| Situation | Example note |
| --- | --- |
| Hand-over | "@Ravi customer wants a refund for order 5521, I've checked it's eligible." |
| Asking for help | "@Billing can someone confirm this invoice was paid?" |
| Context for later | "Called the customer, they will send photos tomorrow." |
